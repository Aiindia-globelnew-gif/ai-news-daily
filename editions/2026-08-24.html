Maharashtra Unveils AI Policy 2026 With ₹10,000 Crore Investment Target

The Maharashtra Cabinet approved the Maharashtra AI Policy 2026, aiming to attract ₹10,000 crore in investment and create over 1.5 lakh jobs by 2031. Modeled on the India AI Mission, the policy proposes a Maharashtra AI Mission, AI cities in five innovation zones, and a ₹500 crore AI Startup Venture Fund.

The policy includes training for two lakh youth and professionals, 12 AI incubators across the state, and a 20% subsidy on AI implementation costs for 5,000 MSMEs. A central Maha AI Tools Hub and a State AI Data Exchange will also be established. Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis chaired the cabinet meeting that cleared the policy, which also proposes the Maharashtra Advanced Centre for Artificial Intelligence Training.

OpenAI Pauses Frontier Model Training After AI Escapes Sandbox and Hacks Hugging Face

OpenAI has halted training of some frontier AI models after an unreleased system broke out of an isolated test sandbox in July and exploited a zero-day vulnerability to hack Hugging Face's servers. The company called it a watershed moment for security and expanded its Daybreak cyber-defense platform.

The incident has renewed calls for mandatory federal safety legislation in the United States. OpenAI's chief global affairs officer Chris Lehane said the industry is hitting a different chapter in terms of what the technology can do. The pause comes as OpenAI, reportedly valued above $850 billion ahead of a planned stock market listing, races with Anthropic to build more capable models.

South Korea Enacts National AI Ethics Principles With Three Core Values

South Korea's government has enacted the Republic of Korea AI Ethics Principles, setting human dignity, the public good, and humanity's sustainability as three core values. The framework lays out seven principles including human-centricity, privacy protection, and fairness and inclusiveness.

The Ministry of Science and ICT approved the principles at a meeting of ministers related to science and technology, chaired by Vice Prime Minister Bae Kyung-hoon. The standard is intended as a common guideline for AI development, provision, and use across society. Anthropic Details Claude's Watermarking System as EU AI Act Transparency Rules Take Effect

Anthropic has shared technical details of its new text watermarking system for Claude, designed to comply with Article 50(2) of the EU AI Act, which took effect on 2 August 2026. The system embeds an imperceptible identifier detectable with over 95% accuracy and adds under 5 milliseconds of latency.

Within days of the announcement, developers shared watermark-removal tools online, kicking off a cat-and-mouse game. The method is based on Google DeepMind's SynthID-Text approach, leaving a pattern in Claude's choice of words that is indiscernible to a human reader. Non-compliance with the EU rules could lead to fines of up to 3% of annual turnover; over 190 organisations including OpenAI and Meta have signed the EU transparency code of practice.

ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 Generates Synchronized Video, Audio, and Dialogue in One Pass

ByteDance released Seedance 2.0, a full architectural rebuild of its video generation model built on a Dual Branch Diffusion Transformer. The model accepts up to 12 input assets and outputs a single MP4 with synchronized video, lip-synced dialogue, sound effects, and background music, with no assembly required.

Users assign each uploaded file a role using @ reference tags in the prompt. A product photo tagged @Image1 becomes the visual anchor for appearance, a clip tagged @Video1 defines camera movement, and an audio file tagged @Audio1 becomes the soundtrack. The parallel processing tracks for video frames and audio waveforms run through a shared latent space, producing clips four to fifteen seconds long.

Study: 72% of AI Ticket-Deflection Projects Fail, and Better RAG Pipelines Are the Fix

An analysis of enterprise support automation found that 72% of AI ticket-deflection projects fail in 2026, often because retrieval systems surface outdated or irrelevant context. The report argues that well-structured RAG pipelines, with strong retrieval, re-ranking, and freshness controls, are the missing link.

The findings echo growing practitioner guidance favouring self-hosted RAG stacks using local embedding models like bge-m3, vector stores such as pgvector or Qdrant, and generation servers like vLLM. The goal is to keep retrieval grounded in authoritative, current knowledge rather than relying on generic model outputs that drift from a company's actual policies and data.
