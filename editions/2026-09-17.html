★ Top Story Global

OpenAI confirms weeks of AI safety coordination with Anthropic and Google DeepMind

OpenAI's global policy chief Chris Lehane disclosed on Tuesday that the three leading frontier labs have spent weeks working together on AI safety — an unusual collaboration between fierce competitors. The talks centre on shared standards, testing and pre-release evaluation, and follow Dario Amodei's call to "pace the frontier" and Demis Hassabis's July proposal for a FINRA-style standards body. Lehane said no antitrust waiver is needed; sceptics range from the FTC chair to President Trump.

The disclosure reframed what looked like a spontaneous weekend alignment — Amodei's 3,800-word essay, Altman's endorsement and Elon Musk's support — as part of structured, ongoing cross-competitor coordination. OpenAI says it backs bipartisan US proposals including the FRONTIER Act, which would require independent audits of frontier models, and a Senate measure taking shape under John Thune, Ted Cruz and Amy Klobuchar. FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson has reportedly criticised the plan as "moat digging" by incumbents, and President Trump has dismissed AI risk concerns as a hoax — leaving the effort squeezed between competition hawks and safety advocates. No final structure or formal agreement has been announced.

Source: Reuters / Bloomberg via Hindustan Times, 16 September 2026

India

Maharashtra cabinet clears AI Policy 2026 targeting ₹10,000 crore in investment

The Maharashtra cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, approved the Maharashtra AI Policy 2026, to be implemented on the lines of the India AI Mission. It targets ₹10,000 crore in investment and over 1.5 lakh jobs by 2031, backed by a ₹500 crore AI Startup Venture Fund, 12 incubators and "AI cities" in five innovation areas.

The policy also proposes a Maharashtra AI Mission, a State AI Data Exchange and a Maharashtra Advanced Centre for Artificial Intelligence Training, plus a central Maha AI Tools Hub platform. Training is planned for two lakh youth and professionals, and 5,000 MSMEs will get a 20 per cent subsidy on AI implementation costs.

Source: NewsOnAir (Akashvani News), 16 September 2026

Activate closes $105 million maiden fund to back India's next AI startups

Venture firm Activate, founded by Haptik creator Aakrit Vaish and former Together Fund investor Pratyush Choudhury, has closed a $105 million early-stage AI fund. Backers include Vinod Khosla, General Catalyst, Vijay Shekhar Sharma and Ranjan Pai, and the firm has already backed Sarvam AI, ElevenLabs and Wispr Flow.

Activate plans $500,000–$3 million cheques in 25–30 pre-seed and seed startups across three areas: domestic AI capabilities including models, compute and defence; AI services businesses; and consumer applications in commerce, financial services, education and healthcare. "The prevailing narrative is that India is behind in AI; we believe India is simply early," Vaish said.

Salesforce built its own enterprise reasoning model, Koa, with NVIDIA

Salesforce introduced Koa, its first AI reasoning model optimised for enterprise work, post-trained from NVIDIA's open 120-billion-parameter Nemotron 3 Super and running inside the Salesforce trust boundary. The company says early benchmarking on CRM-specific tasks such as updating opportunities and routing cases outperforms the strongest general-purpose models.

Koa joins a portfolio of specialised models including HyperClassifier, TextEval and Moirai, and is already running in internal employee workflows, with customer pilots underway in service, sales and commerce.

Research

ZGCM-1: a fully open 7B model that punches at frontier weight

A new 7B dense foundation model, ZGCM-1, scores 75.0% on AIME 2026 and 63.1% on WebWalkerQA — competitive with models tens of times larger on math and agentic search — using a 256K-token context, hybrid sliding-window attention, FP8 training with the Muon optimizer, and a roughly 4.2x pre-training efficiency gain. Weights, checkpoints, training code, data recipes and logs are all public.

On Binary Function Search — locating a function in a stripped binary from a behavioural description — ZGCM-1 reaches 62%, versus 12% for same-scale Qwen3-8B. The team also ran an "AI-native R&D" workflow in which agent swarms handled data curation, cluster operations and evaluation, with a diagnostic benchmark of 2,503 probes finishing in 2–3 minutes.

Source: arXiv (via ChatPaper), 16 September 2026

Policy

Bruegel: the EU AI Act is now enforceable, but far from reassuring

A Bruegel analysis argues that while the EU AI Act entered its enforcement phase in August 2026 — giving the Commission power to investigate general-purpose AI providers and fine them up to 3% of worldwide revenue — the framework is close to impossible to enforce with current means. The AI Office has roughly 145 staff, most high-risk rules were delayed to December 2027 and August 2028, and no harmonised standards have yet been published.

The think tank calls for stronger post-deployment supervision, routine spot-checks of providers' risk evidence, and an EU liability regime for downstream harms — noting that the withdrawn AI Liability Directive leaves people denied a job or credit having to prove faults inside opaque AI systems in national courts.

Source: Bruegel, 16 September 2026

Frameworks

Google open-sources Agent Substrate for running agent fleets on GKE

Google made Agent Substrate available on Google Kubernetes Engine — an open-source runtime for running large numbers of AI agent sandboxes that separates agent execution from machine management. It is built to work with agent frameworks and harnesses including Claude Code, OpenClaw, Hermes, Antigravity and Codex.

Kubernetes handles the underlying machines while a separate execution layer manages sandbox lifecycles. The software is open source for other Kubernetes environments, though several conveniences — such as custom ComputeClasses for spot and on-demand capacity across processor types — are tied to Google Cloud. General-availability support for production deployments is offered through an allowlist process.

Source: iTNews, 16 September 2026

Pinterest rewrites its vector search stack for tens of billions of embeddings

Pinterest detailed a rebuild of Manas, its vector-search platform deployed across 80 clusters serving Home Feed, Search, Related Pins, Ads and Notifications — moving from memory-hungry HNSW indices to quantized representations and SSD-based SPANN serving. The changes delivered 20–30% serving cost savings while scaling to tens of billions of embeddings.

Scalar quantization kept recall above 90% while shrinking HNSW indices 59%, and a custom SPANN implementation delivered 3x the QPS of DiskANN with one-third the latency in benchmarks, saving over 40% of CPU time versus full in-memory HNSW on a 5-billion-embedding production evaluation. Pinterest is now piloting ColBERT-style late-interaction models for finer-grained relevance.

Source: InfoQ via LavX News, 16 September 2026

ShengShu's Vidu S2 brings real-time interaction and editing to AI video

ShengShu Technology unveiled Vidu S2, pairing Vidu S2-Avatar — a real-time interactive digital-character model that now outputs 720p and accepts new reference images mid-conversation — with Vidu S2-Editing, which restyles, re-dresses or replaces subjects and backgrounds in a live video stream. The company is also exploring real-time spatial video generation for VR headsets.

A vision-language-model agent reads user instructions and checks output frames in chronological order, while a lightweight single-step refiner restores high-resolution detail and TurboDiffusion-style inference techniques keep overhead low. The accompanying paper reports that Vidu S2 outperforms all baselines, and a playable demo is available at vidu.com.

Vision

Sora's API shuts down next week, closing the book on OpenAI's video app

OpenAI's Sora API is scheduled to shut down on 24 September 2026 — the standalone web and app experiences already closed on 26 April — with user data and generated videos expected to be deleted afterwards. Migration guides now steer creators toward Google Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Runway Gen-4.5 and Dreamina Seedance 2.5.

The wind-down is the clearest sign yet that model capability and product durability are separate attributes in generative video, and it lands amid copyright litigation such as Disney v. Midjourney that is reshaping the closed-model side of the market. The most urgent task for existing Sora users is exporting their libraries before the deletion deadline.

Source: Yasue / OpenAI Help Center, 16 September 2026
