Uttar Pradesh Unveils Startup Policy 2026 with ₹1,000 Crore Fund and Deep-Tech Patience Capital

The UP government's new five-year startup policy establishes a ₹1,000 crore fund-of-funds, expands the AKTU corpus to ₹400 crore, and introduces a "Patience Capital" mechanism offering up to ₹40 crore per deep-tech startup. The policy also plans 20 new Centres of Excellence in AI, quantum computing, and space-tech.

The policy, issued by the Department of IT & Electronics, goes beyond conventional incentives. Up to four deep-tech startups per year can receive patience capital of up to ₹40 crore each, with an annual cap of ₹100 crore, targeting ventures that need longer R&D cycles before commercialisation. A UP Investor Network aims to aggregate up to ₹100 crore from angels and VCs for startups in AI, ML, EVs, MedTech, robotics and drones. The framework also proposes a U-Hub incubation centre under StartinUP and capital-expenditure support of up to ₹1.50 crore for incubators in Purvanchal and Bundelkhand.

Anthropic Ships Claude Fable 5.1, Topping the AI Intelligence Rankings

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 on 1 September, scoring 66 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — the highest measured — ahead of Claude Opus 5 (63), GPT-5.6 Sol (61), and Grok 4.6 (61). The model ships with a 75% cut to cache-read pricing and a new enterprise safety architecture.

Fable 5.1 scored 52.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, more than double Fable 5's 24.7%. A restricted sibling, Mythos 5.1, retains full cyber and bio capabilities for vetted organisations through Project Glasswing. Anthropic also introduced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS), letting customers keep agent-monitoring data inside their own infrastructure. Base pricing remains $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, but cache reads dropped from $1.00 to $0.25 per million.

Google Releases TimesFM-3, a Multivariate Time-Series Foundation Model

Google Research introduced TimesFM-3, a 330-million-parameter foundation model pre-trained on over 1 trillion time points that performs multivariate forecasting in a single forward pass. It tops the Gift-Eval, FEV-Bench, and Time benchmarks for both point and probabilistic forecasting.

Unlike its univariate-only predecessors, TimesFM-3 natively captures dependencies between co-evolving time series, supporting covariates and cross-series information without task-specific fine-tuning. Even in univariate mode it matches or beats competing models, and full multivariate mode delivers a further leap. The model is available now on GitHub and Hugging Face, with BigQuery integration coming in weeks.

Weaviate Introduces Late-Interaction Multi-Vector Retrieval for Chart-Heavy PDFs

Weaviate detailed a new approach to RAG over PDFs using late-interaction multi-vector models that embed entire page images — not extracted text — enabling retrieval of charts, tables, and layouts by visual meaning. The method skips OCR and chunking entirely for image-rich documents.

The hosted multi2multivec-weaviate module encodes each page as hundreds of vectors, compressed for storage efficiency. Demonstrated against NVIDIA's FY2026 earnings, the system retrieves the exact chart answering a question and the Weaviate Query Agent synthesises answers with inline page-image citations. For text-dominated corpora, traditional text embeddings remain cheaper and equally accurate.

India's Copyright Office Rules AI Cannot Be an Author, But AI Art Can Be Copyrighted

India's Copyright Office refused to register an artwork naming the DABUS AI system as author, holding that only a "person who causes the work to be created" qualifies under Section 2(d)(vi). Notably, the Office found the artwork itself meets the originality threshold, meaning AI-generated works can be copyrighted — just not with AI as author.

Registrar Prof. Unnat P. Pandit ruled that "autonomy in execution is not synonymous with conception of a work" and that extending legal personhood to AI "remains a policy decision strictly reserved for Parliament." The decision leaves open how much human involvement suffices for commercial generative-AI outputs — the question DPIIT's committee had set aside for Part II of its working paper.

Source: MediaNama / SCC Times, 1 September 2026

OpenAI Prepares Astra, Its First Model at "Critical" Cyber Capability

OpenAI announced that its forthcoming model Astra is the first to reach its "critical cybersecurity threshold," meaning the model can find and exploit cybersecurity gaps. Google Launches Agentic Video Understanding in Gemini, Cutting Token Use by 88%

Google introduced agentic video understanding across Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash, and 3.5 Flash-Lite, pairing the model's reasoning with native video tools to dynamically search, scan, and inspect video segments. It enables sub-second moment retrieval, long-form needle-in-haystack search, and precise anomaly detection. The feature will also power YouTube's "Ask YouTube" in coming months and rolls out to the Gemini app soon.
