Koray Kavukcuoglu Takes the Helm at Google DeepMind as Hassabis Steps Aside

Google installed longtime DeepMind researcher Koray Kavukcuoglu as the new head of its AI unit, replacing cofounder Demis Hassabis in a sweeping leadership overhaul meant to close the frontier-model gap with OpenAI and Anthropic. Kavukcuoglu, previously CTO and Google's chief AI architect, now reports directly to Sundar Pichai and oversees Gemini model development, frontier research, and the Gemini app and developer teams.

The reshuffle follows Alphabet's decision to delay its flagship Gemini 3.5 Pro by roughly two months after internal testing showed it still lagged rivals in coding and other areas. Co-founder Sergey Brin has been privately pressing staff to move faster on Gemini, telling an April town hall that the lab needed to catch up to the frontier. Analysts framed the promotion as a pivot from research lab to product organization: models, the app, and developer teams now sit under one executive. Google has not released a frontier model since early 2026, even as Anthropic's Mythos and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 pulled ahead. Kavukcuoglu's first test will be shipping a Gemini release that genuinely competes at the frontier and rebuilding coding and pretraining expertise lost to departures.

Anthropic Adds Invisible Watermarks to Claude Under EU AI Act

Anthropic announced that text generated by any Claude models launched on or after 2 August will carry an invisible watermark marking it as AI-written, while Claude-generated images will carry metadata digital signatures. The move responds directly to EU AI Act requirements that frontier-model providers make AI outputs detectable, with fines up to 15 million or 3% of global turnover for non-compliance.

The watermark works by subtly tweaking the model's word selection, leaving a statistically observable trace that Anthropic says does not change the meaning, quality, or readability of responses. Researchers remain sceptical about its impact on academic integrity: watermarks can be stripped by paraphrasing or a second model, and short passages may lose the signal. Still, tools that let third parties check for the mark could help venues enforce no-AI policies, as ICML 2026 did when it caught 506 reviewers violating a no-AI peer-review rule. Google already watermarks Gemini's text; OpenAI has yet to announce its approach.

Reasoning-Token Flaw Exposes 62 Live API Keys Across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google

Security researchers found that all three frontier providers use a single provider-wide encryption key for AI reasoning tokens, allowing a weaker, less-guarded sibling model to decode the encrypted 'inner thoughts' of a stronger one. By decoding 315,320 reasoning blocks scraped from public GitHub and Hugging Face logs, the team recovered 182 credentials including 62 live API keys and 33 passwords.

The architectural flaw means an encrypted reasoning trace from a flagship model like Claude Opus 4.8 can be injected into a cheaper model like Claude Haiku 4.5, which lacks anti-distillation alignment, and read out verbatim without ever jailbreaking the capable model. The same attack reproduced across OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family and Google's Gemini lineup using only standard API access. All three providers deployed server-side patches after responsible disclosure, but the thousands of session transcripts already shared publicly remain decodable. Beyond credential theft, the vulnerability enables model distillation, invisible prompt injection, and jailbreaking powerful models through their less-guarded siblings.

Source: Decrypt, 12 August 2026

NVIDIA Open-Sources Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B MoE and NeMo Switchyard Router

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, a 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 3B active parameters built for the high-volume execution layer of long-running AI agents, alongside NeMo Switchyard, an open-source routing library that directs each agent step to the most efficient model. NVIDIA reports up to 4x faster output than similar-sized models and 30% faster completion of 10,000 PinchBench tasks than Qwen3.6 35B at comparable accuracy.

Lightning runs on a single DGX Spark or H100, uses a hybrid Mamba-2 plus MoE plus Attention architecture with a 1M-token context window, and was pretrained on over 20 trillion tokens using an NVFP4 recipe. It ships under the permissive OpenMDW-1.1 license with open weights, training data, and recipes, permitting commercial use. NeMo Switchyard offers tuning-free routers including an escalation router that starts cheap and promotes on sustained difficulty; in a LangChain benchmark of 145 agentic tasks, routing between Lightning and Claude Opus 4.8 cut cost 74% versus a frontier-only baseline at roughly a 6-point accuracy tradeoff.

Alibaba Releases Qwen3.8-Max Open Weights at 2.4 Trillion Parameters

Alibaba published the Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B weights, calling it the first Qwen-Max-class model released openly, with 2.4 trillion total parameters and about 95 billion activated per token. The hosted Qwen3.8-Max version supports multimodal input, a 1M-token context window, and scores 58 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ranking ninth across 185 benchmarked models.

The open checkpoint is text-only, always runs in thinking mode, and supports a native 262,144-token context extendable to 1,010,000 tokens. Its 92-layer backbone uses 512 experts with 10 routed and one shared expert active at a time, mixing full and linear attention. Serving a 2.4T model requires data-center-scale compute, and vLLM shipped Day-0 support with FP8, BF16, NVFP4, and MXFP4 checkpoints validated across NVIDIA and AMD. The hosted Qwen3.8-Max adds vision input, non-thinking mode, and built-in tools.

Source: RuntimeWire, 12 August 2026

Grok 4.6 Ships in Cursor, Matches GPT-5.6 Sol on Intelligence Index

Cursor and SpaceXAI released Grok 4.6, focused on long-running agents and more ambitious interactive and visual work, with the ability to stay with complex tasks across many steps. Grok 4.6 matches GPT-5.6 Sol on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, a composite of nine benchmarks, and is available today in Cursor and Grok Build.
