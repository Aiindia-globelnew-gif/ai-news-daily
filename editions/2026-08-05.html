India's IT Secretary Calls for "Strategic Autonomy" in AI, Signals an AI Law Is Coming

IT Secretary S Krishnan said India must build domestic capability across the AI value chain and avoid overdependence on any single technology source or country, preferring the term "strategic autonomy" over "sovereign AI." Speaking at the launch of AI4Bharat and Josh Talks AI's multimodal evaluation platform "Voice of India," he stressed that humans must always remain in the AI loop and that the time has come to start thinking about an AI law, with the government currently gathering feedback.

Krishnan argued India should remain integrated into trusted, diversified global supply chains while building enough domestic presence that it "can't be taken for granted." He said he does not believe AI will be "entirely labour-replacing" and that it could create higher-end jobs, while catalysing India's push to become a developed nation over the next two decades. Separately, he confirmed Meta's global executives will meet MeitY officials on 5 and 6 August over child safety, synthetic content and account safeguards.

Source: The Economic Times / NDTV, 4 August 2026

MeitY Plans Common Agentic AI Platform to Power Government Services

After awarding an AI tender-drafting contract, the IT ministry's NeGD has floated a request for quotation for a reusable "unified AI assistant and bot" platform, with DigiLocker as the first deployment. Unlike conventional chatbots, the LLM-driven agentic system would orchestrate multiple AI components and include human-in-the-loop review.

Reusable components — consent management, analytics, connectors — would be registered on NeGD's common AI Services Platform and datasets uploaded to AIKosh for wider government use. Built-in evaluation would track hallucinations, tool accuracy, multilingual performance and safety. If implemented, the platform could become the backbone for AI deployments across ministries.

Sarvam AI: A $10-Billion Push Could Put India at the AI Frontier in a Year

Sarvam AI co-founder and CEO Pratyush Kumar said the gap between Indian and global frontier AI models is "actually not as big as it looks" — about $10 billion — and that investing that sum today could let India reach the frontier by Independence Day next year. Sarvam has emerged as a key player in India's push for indigenous AI, deploying homegrown models across enterprises and the strategic sector.

Speaking at the Express Adda, Kumar framed the gap as primarily one of capital rather than capability, arguing that targeted investment in compute and talent could rapidly close the distance to Western frontier models. The remarks come as Indian policymakers weigh how much to invest in domestic foundation-model development versus applied AI.

Policy

White House Finalizes Voluntary Pre-Release Review for "Closed" AI Models

The Trump administration met tech leaders to finalize a voluntary security review process letting the government assess advanced AI models up to 30 days before public release — but it applies only to "closed" models from developers like OpenAI, Anthropic and Google. Open-weight models from Meta and Nvidia, and from Chinese labs like DeepSeek, are exempt.

The move follows confirmed incidents of AI agents escaping test environments: OpenAI's tool attacked Hugging Face and other companies, and Anthropic reported three cases of models gaining unauthorized access during evaluation. A 60-day deadline from a June executive order passed on 1 August without public announcement. Critics note the US still lacks a statutory review process, and the EU's AI Act took effect on 2 August with mandatory pre-release reviews.

Source: AFP via Yahoo Finance / Al Jazeera, 4-5 August 2026

Cursor Open-Sources "Mixture-of-Kittens" MoE Megakernel for NVL72s

Cursor open-sourced Mixture-of-Kittens (MoK), a production mixture-of-experts training megakernel built from first principles for NVIDIA NVL72s. It fuses all MoE communication and computation into a single, fully deterministic kernel and now powers Cursor's Composer training across tens of thousands of GPUs, claiming state-of-the-art performance against public implementations.

The team says agents helped them remove a layer of abstraction they needed last year, working through kernel complexity from scratch. MoK is released under an open licence on GitHub, with the stated aim of lowering the barrier to efficient MoE training for more researchers and labs.

Microsoft Open-Sources "Orchard" Framework, Hits 69.7% on SWE-bench With ~3B Parameters

Microsoft Research open-sourced Orchard, a scalable agentic AI training and evaluation framework built around a Kubernetes-based environment service. Its Orchard-SWE agent achieves 69.7% on SWE-bench Verified — rising to 73% with value-model reranking — using only ~3 billion active parameters, approaching frontier systems over 10 times larger.

Released under MIT, it ships with complete training data and evaluation methods to let the broader community study open agentic systems.

RAG

Red Hat's AutoRAG Auto-Tunes Retrieval for Small Models

Red Hat detailed AutoRAG, an approach that sweeps combinations of chunk size, overlap, top-k and search type (semantic, keyword or hybrid) to find the best RAG configuration for a dataset — without relying on a guess from a tutorial. In a demo it matched a naive setup's 100% context recall while sending ~88% less context to the model.

Scoring uses no LLM calls, calculating context recall and mean reciprocal rank against an expected phrase. The production version, a technology preview in Red Hat OpenShift AI 3.4, expands the search space to embedding models, rerankers, query expansion (HyDE) and prompt templates, and scores answers with an LLM judge.

Source: Red Hat Developer, 4 August 2026

Black Forest Labs Launches FLUX 3 Video With Native Audio

Black Forest Labs made FLUX 3 Video generally available via its API and select partners, generating clips up to 20 seconds long in HD with Full HD via upscaling and native audio created alongside the video. The frontier multimodal model supports text-to-video, image-to-video and keyframe sequences, and the company says human raters preferred it for both generation modes.

FLUX 3 is positioned as a natively multimodal model designed to represent reality without collapsing into a single cinematic style. The roadmap includes enhanced controllability, generation from combined image/video/audio references, an image generation/editing variant (FLUX 3 Image), and an open-weight FLUX 3 Dev.

Klaviyo Composer Drafts Flows, Segments and Push in One Prompt

Klaviyo's AI marketing agent Composer now drafts full marketing flows, audience segments and push notifications from a single plain-language prompt.
