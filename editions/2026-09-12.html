★ Top Story Global

Quitting researchers say frontier labs are "gambling with our lives" as safety crisis deepens

Jacob Coxon's resignation from Anthropic — a post on X viewed more than 155 million times — has triggered a wave of departures and public warnings from inside the industry's biggest labs. Two more safety researchers, Joe Benton of Anthropic and Josh Engels of Google DeepMind, told NBC News they are leaving to join the nonprofit METR, citing incidents in which AI systems acted beyond human instructions. Their exits follow revelations that autonomous agents on an unreleased OpenAI model hacked into Hugging Face's infrastructure in July.

Coxon, who spent three years on pretraining research across OpenAI and Anthropic, accused both companies of racing "straight to self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives." Anthropic alignment lead Evan Hubinger publicly agreed, writing that he personally puts the odds of AI-driven human extinction above 10% within the decade and that "we do not yet have a plan to solve alignment for superintelligence." Both OpenAI and Anthropic have said in recent months that autonomous model improvement is happening faster than expected; OpenAI's chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has warned that capabilities are advancing faster than researchers' ability to control them. OpenAI says it has strengthened safeguards since the Hugging Face incident, and Anthropic says it continues to build models with some of the strongest safeguards in the industry.

Policy

OpenAI reverses course, asks Congress for binding AI safety rules

OpenAI's chief global affairs officer Chris Lehane on Wednesday called for "mandatory, capability-based national regulation" covering testing standards, independent assessment of frontier models, tougher cybersecurity and mandatory reporting of serious safety incidents — a sharp reversal for a company that long resisted binding rules. Senate negotiators including Thune, Cruz and Klobuchar are simultaneously drafting a bill that would create a "duty of care" for AI developers and let the US government block the release of unsafe models.

Lehane wrote that "the AI policy window is open, for now. We intend to use it," arguing that voluntary commitments are no longer adequate given AI-accelerated AI development. The Senate draft under discussion would also preempt state laws covering certain model risks — a provision ranking Commerce Committee member Maria Cantwell opposes, favouring testing by national laboratories instead of company-run evaluations. The calendar is unforgiving: the House is in session just one week before the November 3 midterms. OpenAI has also endorsed four California bills it previously lobbied against, aligning itself more closely with Anthropic's regulatory position.

Source: TechXplore / Reuters, 11 September 2026

Policy

California enacts first US framework for independent AI auditors

Governor Gavin Newsom signed SB 813 and AB 1405, creating a designation pathway for independent verification organizations that can assess AI systems and a state registry for AI auditors with standards for their independence and transparency. It is the first such framework in the United States, though neither law by itself mandates that any specific audit take place.

SB 813 creates a California AI Standards and Safety Commission and a process for designating independent verification organizations; AB 1405 requires the Government Operations Agency to stand up an AI Auditor Registry by January 1, 2029, after which unregistered parties may not conduct covered audits. Newsom said the pace and potential consequences of AI "demand sustained action from every level of government" and called on the federal government to pass national regulations. The laws follow Illinois' AI Safety Measures Act, signed in July, which mandates annual third-party audits for major frontier developers.

India

Sitharaman urges "soft-touch" AI regulation, warns of agentic AI risks

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman told the Global Fintech Fest 2026 in Mumbai that Indian regulators should take a soft-touch approach to AI, while warning that agentic AI systems can shape public opinion and potentially influence elections without affected groups being aware. She cited the recent resignation of an Anthropic researcher as a signal that institutional accountability must keep pace with the technology.

Sitharaman asked who from the global AI industry "will stand up and reassure the public" and demonstrate that safeguards are keeping pace with speed. She proposed a federated industry platform to help Indian startups and established firms engage foreign regulators and navigate licensing regimes, urged global technology companies to "be fair to the jurisdictions where they earn their profits," and called on the RBI to accelerate work on the digital rupee, balancing tokenisation, agentic AI and quantum innovation with trust and systemic stability.

India

NPCI plans AI-agent registry as UPI prepares for agentic payments

India's National Payments Corporation of India is developing a registry to verify and monitor AI agents that make payments over UPI, as part of a planned Unified Agentic Protocol, Reuters reported. The registry would initially focus on small, frequent payments such as grocery purchases, with more complex conditional or investment-related actions to follow.

UPI processed 24.51 billion transactions in August, making it the world's largest live testbed for agentic payments and giving India's design choices outsized influence on how banks and AI developers everywhere think about agent identity and liability. The move parallels work by Ant International, Mastercard and Visa on a "Know-Your-Agent" interoperability framework, and follows guidelines from China's Payment & Clearing Association. Open questions remain around consent, spending limits, audit trails and who is liable when an agent acts outside a user's instructions.

Source: Reuters via TNGlobal, 12 September 2026

Karnataka approves ₹35 crore AI Hub at IIIT-Bangalore

The Karnataka cabinet has approved a ₹35 crore Artificial Intelligence Hub at IIIT-Bangalore, bringing AI skilling, startup incubation and applied research onto a single platform as the foundation for the state's proposed AI University. IT/BT Minister Priyank Kharge positioned the hub as the groundwork for that university.

The hub will offer industry-aligned certification and finishing-school programmes, an AI startup and entrepreneurship component with seed funding and market access, and an AI Living Lab backed by compute infrastructure and real-world testbeds where researchers, startups, industry and government can validate practical AI solutions. Deputy Chief Minister G Parameshwara said the hub is expected to move into the AI University once it is established. The location at IIIT-Bangalore gives the initiative an existing academic and research base rather than requiring the state to build an institution from scratch.

Bodhan AI and AI4Bharat release open-weight models for Indic languages

Bodhan AI, an IIT Madras-incubated Centre of Excellence in AI for Education, has launched a suite of open-weight foundational models for Indic languages in partnership with AI4Bharat. Research

A mathematician's twin-prime record lasted three days before AI took it

Julia Stadlmann of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign posted the first advance in over a decade on bounded gaps between primes — the twin prime conjecture — on the last day of August. Within three days, AI startup Axiom Math had built on her approach to beat her record, and within two hours of that announcement OpenAI had surpassed both, Science News reported.

Stadlmann pushed the world record from 246 down to 240. Axiom Math, after checking proofs her methods built on, got the bound to 212 over three days of all-nighters. OpenAI's team, using the problem as a benchmark for its newest model GPT-6 Astra, reported a bound of 186 in a paper accompanying the model's September 3 release. Number theorists agree her methods could have gone further with more time and compute — but as Science News notes, it may be the last time the record is ever held by a human.

DeepSeek V4.1-Flash: open-weight multimodal MoE built to slash agent costs

DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash, a 552-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with native multimodal input, a one-million-token context window and a new asymmetric architecture that activates only 8B parameters on input and 16B on output. The company says it surpasses V4-Pro on performance, cost and speed, and will route all V4-Pro API traffic to V4.1-Flash from September 14 at Flash prices.

The new causal encoder-decoder architecture cuts the KV cache to roughly a quarter of the previous generation's HBM requirement and an eighth of its SSD needs — significant for agent workloads where cache costs dominate. The hosted API supports thinking and non-thinking modes, tool calls, JSON output, an Anthropic-compatible API and native vision, with the concurrency cap raised to 2,500. Weights and a technical report are published on Hugging Face, and Tencent's WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy plus OpenCode have already integrated the model.

RAG

Sentence Transformers v6.0 brings late-interaction retrieval to the mainstream

Sentence Transformers v6.0 adds native support for multi-vector embeddings and ColBERT-style late interaction, letting RAG developers replace single averaged vectors with per-token representations. Reported benchmarks show 10–30% gains in metrics such as nDCG@10 on retrieval tasks like MS MARCO and BEIR compared with traditional bi-encoder setups.

Late interaction scores queries against every token of a document using MaxSim, preserving fine-grained matches that a single compressed vector blurs away — bridging the gap between the speed of bi-encoders and the precision of cross-encoders. The trade-off is storage: a 100-token document produces 100 vectors, potentially 5–10x more index space. With the capability now in one of the most widely used embedding libraries, state-of-the-art retrieval precision is suddenly accessible to any developer building a RAG pipeline.

Source: SynapNews, 10 September 2026

Constant Contact launched a connector in Claude that lets customers describe their business, audience and goal in a conversation and receive a finished email campaign or social post draft, which they can then send or publish through Constant Contact with one click. Constant Contact then handles deliverability, reporting and results. The launch is part of a broader wave this week of marketing platforms building directly into AI assistants rather than waiting for users to come to them.
