OpenAI's AI agents solve the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem in 88 hours

An internal OpenAI system — reportedly more capable than GPT-6 Astra — produced a Lean-formalized proof that the Navier–Stokes fluid equations can 'blow up' in finite time, resolving one of the Clay Mathematics Institute's $1 million Millennium Prize problems.

A swarm of roughly 10,000 autonomous agents exchanged nearly 3 million messages and consumed about 130 billion output tokens over 88 hours to reach the result, then formalized it in the Lean proof assistant — a step estimated to cost around $15 million commercially. The announcement was shadowed by a priority dispute: NYU's Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic's Levent Alpöge released related Euler-equation results the previous night and questioned OpenAI's timing. OpenAI says it did not access their work and will not claim the prize.

Source: Nature & The Guardian, 8 September 2026

DeepMind's AlphaGenome Atlas maps all 9 billion human DNA variants

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas, a 1-petabyte catalogue predicting the regulatory impact of every possible single-letter DNA change in the human genome, paired with a single AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score.

The Atlas combines AlphaGenome and AlphaMissense into one ranking number, helping researchers prioritize variants in rare-disease and complex-trait studies. It is free for non-commercial academic use through a no-code web portal, with commercial access on Google Cloud coming soon.

Source: Google DeepMind, 8 September 2026

OpenAI rolled out ChatGPT Images 2.5 across all tiers, adding sketch-based generation, templates, better likeness preservation, and up to 50% lower latency, with two new API models — Flare and Sunburst.

Vision

NVIDIA and SANA's Sol-H3 generates 5-second video in 1.65 seconds — faster than playback

A new end-to-end inference stack for the MiniMax-H3 open-source video model produces a 5-second, 1344×768 clip with stereo audio in 1.653 seconds on 8× NVIDIA B300 GPUs — shorter than the clip itself.

Combining NVIDIA's Sol-Attn sparse attention with MiniMax's four-step distillation, Sol-H3 delivers an 11x speedup over the base 50-step model and is released under Apache 2.0. The team says it clears the threshold for real-time, interactive AI video.

Source: NVIDIA / SANA, 7 September 2026

China's Supreme Court issues first national judicial rules on AI disputes

The Supreme People's Court released 24 articles guiding lower courts on AI civil and criminal disputes, the first such national rules, while sidestepping the question of whether AI-generated works are copyrightable.

Framed as guidance applying existing law (Civil Code, Cybersecurity Law, Copyright Law, etc.) since China has no dedicated AI statute, the rules call for higher courts to hear significant or novel cases and for inter-departmental governance collaboration.
