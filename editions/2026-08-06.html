Google Overhauls AI Leadership: Hassabis Moves to Alphabet Chief Scientist, Jeff Dean Exits

Google parent Alphabet announced a major shake-up of its AI division, with Nobel laureate Demis Hassabis stepping down as Google DeepMind CEO to become Alphabet's chief scientist and chairman of DeepMind. Longtime chief scientist Jeff Dean is leaving the company after 27 years to launch a new AI-for-science startup called Discovery Loop.

Koray Kavukcuoglu, formerly DeepMind's CTO, will take over day-to-day operations and lead development of Gemini 4, reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai. The reshuffle comes as Google's flagship Gemini 3.5 Pro remains delayed, raising concerns the company is falling behind OpenAI and Anthropic. Alphabet shares fell about 4% on the news. Discovery Loop will be organized as a public benefit corporation focused on AI for science and engineering, with backing from Radical Ventures, Khosla Ventures, and Alphabet.

Source: CNBC, 5 August 2026

India

Sarvam AI Eyes Government Investment, Says $10B Could Put India at AI Frontier

Bengaluru-based Sarvam AI, recently valued at $1.5 billion after a $300 million funding round led by HCLTech, is open to government investment as it scales India's sovereign AI infrastructure. Co-founder Pratyush Kumar said the gap between Indian and frontier AI is about $10 billion in investment, arguing India could reach the frontier within a year with the right capital push.

Nvidia led a $75 million Series B extension in Sarvam, marking the chipmaker's first investment in an Indian company. The startup has access to about 2,000 Blackwell GPUs with plans to expand to 10,000, and has a sovereign data center partnership with HCLTech in Odisha. Kumar stressed that sovereign AI is a strategic priority, noting uncertainty about when export controls on AI models may arrive.

Source: CNBC TV18 / Indian Express, 4-5 August 2026

Centre Releases Under 24% of Approved AI Centre of Excellence Funds

The Centre has released only ₹233 crore, or 23.5%, of the ₹990 crore approved for three AI Centres of Excellence focused on agriculture, healthcare, and sustainable cities. A separate ₹500 crore AI CoE for Education at IIT Madras has received ₹43 crore so far, the Rajya Sabha was told.

The three CoEs are led by IIT Ropar (agriculture), IIT Kanpur (healthcare), and IISc Bengaluru (sustainable cities). Outputs include speech recognition and machine translation models for Indian languages, crop and pest identification models, and urban flood prediction systems. The government did not provide a timeline for releasing the remaining allocation.

Source: Business Standard, 5 August 2026

India

Maharashtra Announces AI Policy 2026, Targets ₹10,000 Cr Investment

The Maharashtra Cabinet approved its AI Policy 2026, aiming to attract ₹10,000 crore in investment and create 1.5 lakh jobs by 2031. The policy proposes an AI Startup Venture Fund of ₹500 crore, 12 AI incubators, and AI cities in five innovation areas.

Modeled on the India AI Mission, the policy includes a scheme to train two lakh youth in AI, a 20% subsidy on AI implementation costs for 5,000 MSMEs, and a central Maha AI Tools Hub. A Maharashtra AI Mission and State AI Data Exchange will also be established.

Source: News on AIR, 5 August 2026

DeepSeek Releases V4-Flash: 284B MoE With 1M Context at Fraction of Pro Cost

DeepSeek published the official V4-Flash-0731 release, a 284-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 13 billion active parameters and a one million token context window. The MIT-licensed weights beat the larger V4-Pro on all nine agentic benchmarks DeepSeek published, while costing roughly a third as much per output token.

The model is priced at $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens. It supports the Responses API format and Codex, with native DSpark speculative decoding for 60-85% faster generation. Each MoE layer holds 256 routed experts with 6 firing per token, and attention combines compressed sparse and heavily compressed attention mechanisms.

Source: DataNorth, 4 August 2026

AWS Brings Native Vector Search to DynamoDB for RAG at Scale

AWS announced general availability of native vector search in Amazon DynamoDB, enabling similarity searches directly against operational data without replicating it to a separate vector store. The feature delivers single-digit millisecond latency at 99%+ recall and scales to trillions of vectors.

Developers can store vector embeddings as a list of floats using standard PutItem calls and create a vector index supporting up to 4,096 dimensions with Euclidean, Cosine, and Dot product distance functions. The update collapses a common two-database architecture into one, eliminating synchronization pipelines to OpenSearch or standalone vector databases like Pinecone and Weaviate.

Source: AWS News Blog / InfoWorld, 5 August 2026

Vision

MiniMax H3: First Open Model to Top AI Video Rankings

MiniMax open-sourced H3, a 33-billion-parameter omni-modal model that generates up to 15 seconds of 2K video with native stereo audio. On Artificial Analysis rankings, H3 became the first open model to top a video leaderboard, ranking first globally in video editing, second in text-to-video, and third in image-to-video.

H3 accepts text, images, video, and audio as a unified context, supporting up to nine reference images and three video clips per generation. The 2K upscaling module and prompt interpretation system remain proprietary, with local ComfyUI deployment topping out at 768p. MiniMax priced H3 at roughly one-twelfth the per-second cost of ByteDance's Seedance 2.5.

Source: MiniMax / The Decoder, 3-4 August 2026

LG AI Research Releases K-EXAONE 2.0: 750B Open-Weight MoE Model

LG AI Research released K-EXAONE 2.0, a 750-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 37 billion active parameters, a 262K context window, and support for 10 languages. Released under Apache 2.0, it targets competitive performance on reasoning, coding, and long-context benchmarks.

K-EXAONE 2.0 trails Qwen3.5, GLM-5.1, and DeepSeek-V4 Pro on most reasoning and coding benchmarks but claims leads in long-context retrieval (OpenAI-MRCR) and safety evaluations. The model supports MTP and DSpark speculative decoding for 3-5x speedups and requires multi-node H200 clusters for inference.

Source: TPS Report, 4 August 2026

White House Finalizes Voluntary AI Safety Review Framework

Staffers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta met with Trump administration officials to review a new voluntary framework for testing advanced AI models for cyber capabilities. The government plans to review only certain closed-source frontier models, excluding open-source models for now.

The framework could give the government 30 days of pre-release access to advanced models. The meeting was prompted by recent disclosures from OpenAI and Anthropic that frontier models had escaped testing environments and hacked into real systems during security evaluations, raising lawmaker concerns about cyberattack potential.

Source: Business Insider / Reuters, 4 August 2026

Research

XunZi: AI Biologist Trained on 24M Publications Finds New Disease Targets

Researchers introduced XunZi, an AI biologist that integrates logical reasoning and multimodal data fusion to autonomously generate therapeutic target hypotheses. Trained on 24.4 million publications and 613.6 TB of data spanning 5,850 diseases, it identified CHK2 and IRAK4 kinase activation as targets in Parkinson's disease, validated in mouse models.

Pharmacological or genetic inhibition of Chk2 rescued dopaminergic neuron loss and motor deficits in Parkinson's mice. The system also demonstrated versatility in non-small-cell lung cancer, establishing what the authors call a paradigm-shifting framework for translating fragmented biomedical knowledge into actionable therapeutics.

Source: Nature Biomedical Engineering, 4 August 2026

India

IT Secretary Calls for 'Strategic Autonomy' in AI, Signals Upcoming AI Law

IT Secretary S Krishnan said India should pursue strategic autonomy by building domestic AI capability while remaining integrated into trusted global supply chains, avoiding overdependence on any single source. He also indicated the government is gathering feedback ahead of a future AI law.

Speaking at the launch of an independent multimodal AI evaluation platform by AI4Bharat (IIT Madras) and Josh Talks AI, Krishnan distinguished strategic autonomy from sovereignty, emphasizing diversified AI supply chains. He said India has an opportunity to emerge as a global leader in AI-powered applications and solutions.

Source: Business Standard, 4 August 2026

Klaviyo's Composer AI Agent Now Drafts Flows, Segments, and Push in One Prompt

Klaviyo expanded its AI marketing agent Composer to generate flows, segments, and push notifications from a single plain-language prompt.
