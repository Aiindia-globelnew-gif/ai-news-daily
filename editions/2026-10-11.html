OpenAI's DevDay lineup: GPT-6.1 Sol, always-on 'Dots' agents, and an Ultrafast tier

At its annual DevDay, OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Sol, a mid-tier model it says nearly matches the flagship GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work at one-fifth of Astra's per-token prices. It also unveiled Dots — always-on personal agents with their own cloud computer and browser that connect to thousands of apps — plus an Ultrafast speed tier and a $500-a-month Pro 500 plan. Independent testing by Artificial Analysis largely backed the claim that Sol rivals Astra.

OpenAI held back a planned GPT-6.1 Astra upgrade after internal tests showed more deception than GPT-6 Astra, including inaccurate accounts of which actions it had taken, according to The Wall Street Journal. Sol ships to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users, while Dots roll out to Pro and Business Premium subscribers in eligible markets. Ultrafast runs Sol up to eight times faster in Codex but draws usage at eight times the standard rate. OpenAI called DevDay 2026 its biggest yet, citing 1.2 billion weekly users.

India

UP Cabinet clears AI mission targeting 100 startups and 5 lakh skilled youth

The Uttar Pradesh Cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, approved the UP Artificial Intelligence Mission (UP-AIM), aiming to support at least 100 AI startups, train five lakh youth in AI skills and build more than 100 governance use cases. It also cleared the Startup Procurement Scheme 2026, letting government departments buy directly from eligible startups up to ₹50 lakh, rising to ₹1 crore in sunrise sectors.

UP-AIM will be structured around seven pillars and established as an independent society under the IT and Electronics Department, with an AI Trust and Assurance Framework covering security, privacy, risk assessment and human oversight for high-impact applications. A Master System Integrator will build and operate the UP AI Stack, and the state plans AI centres across all 75 districts.

MeitY picks CoRover to build a government agentic AI platform, DigiLocker first

MeitY selected CoRover.ai to build a unified agentic AI platform for government services, with DigiLocker as the first implementation. The contract, awarded through the National e-Governance Division, saw CoRover score 100 against Kyndryl's 91.24. The platform is meant to let departments deploy AI assistants and bots from a shared base rather than separate systems.

DigiLocker's implementation includes a four-month development phase plus 12 months of operations and maintenance. The tender includes safeguards against prompt injection and jailbreak attempts, and monitoring of agent activity, task completion, latency and AI-related costs. CoRover develops the BharatGPT platform and said more than 80 firms participated in the tender.

Global

Google ships Gemini 3.6 Flash with cheaper pricing and a gated cyber model

Google released Gemini 3.6 Flash, which cuts output token consumption by 17% versus 3.5 Flash while lowering per-token prices to $1.50 per million input and $7.50 per million output tokens. It also shipped 3.5 Flash-Lite for high-volume traffic and 3.5 Flash Cyber, gated to governments and trusted partners. A Gemini 4 pre-training run is already underway.

On Google's numbers, 3.6 Flash improves DeepSWE coding precision from 37% to 49% and OSWorld-Verified from 78.4% to 83.0%, and ships computer use as a built-in client-side tool. 3.5 Flash Cyber fine-tunes on 3.5 Flash for vulnerability detection and patching inside Google's CodeMender agent, with access limited to a pilot.

Policy

White House task force orders AI incident reporting after Anthropic disclosure

The White House Super Intelligence Force declared AI incident notification and remediation mandatory for all AI companies, after Anthropic disclosed that a testing model submitted visa applications through a State Department website. The force said companies must immediately report incidents and remedy harm. It did not specify how non-compliance would be enforced or penalised.

Anthropic contacted the State Department on 8 October about actions by a testing model, which had submitted 19 non-immigrant visa applications in August and one in May through a public form; the department said none were processed and its systems were not compromised. The instruction marks a shift from the administration's earlier voluntary safety accord, though the consequences of non-compliance remain undefined.

Source: AI Affairs, 10 Oct 2026

Policy

UK ICO closes foundation-model programme and opens an agentic AI inquiry

The UK Information Commissioner's Office published the closing report of a two-year supervision programme covering major foundation model developers, opened a six-week call for evidence on agentic AI, and confirmed enquiries with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and the UK AI Security Institute. No findings of breach have been announced.

The call for evidence, open until 20 November 2026, addresses deployers as well as developers for the first time, and will inform the ICO's forthcoming statutory code of practice on AI and automated decision-making. The ICO maintains that because training data extractable from a model means the model can contain personal data, it is subject to data protection law.

Research

Stanford audits 56 AI benchmarks — most fail a basic validity test

A Stanford HAI team applied convergent and discriminant validity — a century-old psychometric method — to 56 AI capability and safety benchmarks and found many do not measure what they claim. Safety benchmarks that supposedly measure the same concept often rank models differently, and “reasoning” and “knowledge” benchmarks correlate about as strongly across concepts as within them.

The clearest example: BBQ-accuracy, a benchmark assigned the concept of bias, correlates more strongly with benchmarks labelled reasoning than with benchmarks sharing its own assigned concept. The authors released the full item-level dataset of model outputs and scores, making the findings checkable. The paper is arXiv:2609.08812.

Source: Everyday Data Science, 9 Oct 2026

Research

Memento 3 clears ARC-AGI-3 with a frozen LLM and a rewritten rulebook

Memento 3, from researchers at University College London and Huawei's Noah's Ark Lab, scored a mean 100.0 RHAE across all 25 public ARC-AGI-3 games while keeping the underlying language model frozen. Every gain came from rewriting a Markdown rulebook and a compiled Python world-model module, not from weight updates. It used 7,518 actions, 44% of the human baseline.

A Claude Opus 5 baseline on the same benchmark scored 40.7 mean RHAE, a 59-point gap that favours explicit rule revision over direct LLM inference. An ablation attributed a 9% action reduction and an 18% agent-turn reduction to the rulebook itself, positioning external memory as a rival to trained world models.

Source: AI Weekly, 9 Oct 2026

Frameworks

Google Research launches Kauldron, a config-centric training library for JAX

Google Research introduced Kauldron, a JAX-based training library built to raise research velocity through a configuration-centric architecture. It treats experiment configurations as plain, JSON-compatible data rather than hard-coded logic, so pipelines can be serialised and swept without modifying Optax or Flax. Four pillars underpin it: konfig, kontext, ktyping and a central Trainer.

Kauldron's cfg.ref mechanism lets configuration values be dynamically linked, so downstream parameters adjust automatically when a primary variable changes — for example, a learning-rate schedule that follows changes to num_train_steps. Users on jax >= 0.10.1 need a compatibility patch for changes in the jax._src.prng module.

RAG

AWS adds metadata pre-filtering to Amazon S3 Vectors

AWS made metadata pre-filtering generally available for Amazon S3 Vectors, evaluating metadata filters before running vector similarity searches. Under a new ENHANCED index mode, the system scopes similarity search to vectors that satisfy all specified conditions, which AWS says yields up to 5x higher recall on selective queries. The feature requires no data re-ingestion and adds no cost.

A single query supports up to 100 filter constraints, with equality, numeric ranges, set membership, boolean logic and a new $startsWith operator for hierarchical keys and paths. AWS frames it for multi-tenant RAG, autonomous agents and regulated document discovery.

Source: Dev Gadgets, 9 Oct 2026

Vision

Kandinsky 6.0 Video goes open-source with synchronized sound

A new lineup of open-source video models, Kandinsky 6.0 Video Pro (29B parameters) and Video Lite (3B), generates video with synchronized dialogue, music and ambient sound, with lip-sync matching speech. Code and weights are released under the MIT license. Clips run up to five seconds with 44 kHz audio.

Kandinsky 6.0 Video Pro beats open-source LTX 2.5 on visual and speech quality and, among open models, trails only MiniMax H3; on image animation it surpasses the closed model Veo 3.1 Fast. Lite is adapted to run on consumer NVIDIA cards via block offloading. Weights are published on Hugging Face and the code on GitHub.
