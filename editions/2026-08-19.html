Anthropic Ships Claude Opus 4.7, Narrowly Retaking the Frontier Benchmark Crown

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.7, calling it a notable improvement over Opus 4.6 in advanced software engineering and agentic coding. The model narrowly outscores OpenAI's GPT-5.4 and Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro on key benchmarks, though the lead is tightening.

Pricing is unchanged at US$5 per million input tokens and US$25 per million output tokens, and the model is live across Anthropic's API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI and Microsoft Foundry, plus GitHub Copilot for Pro+, Business and Enterprise users. Opus 4.7 takes the top spot on SWE-bench Pro and SWE-bench Verified; Cursor co-founder Michael Truell reported it cleared 70 per cent on CursorBench versus 58 per cent for Opus 4.6, and XBOW's Oege de Moor cited a jump from 54.5 per cent to 98.5 per cent on the firm's visual-acuity benchmark. Vision also gets a major upgrade - the model processes images up to 2,576 pixels on the long edge, more than three times prior Claude resolution. The subtext: Anthropic repeatedly positions Opus 4.7 as less broadly capable than 'Claude Mythos Preview', a frontier system restricted to roughly 40 vetted enterprise and government partners including Apple, Google, Microsoft and AWS. Opus 4.7 ships with deliberately reduced cyber capabilities and automated safeguards.

Google launched Gemini 3.7 Flash on 13 August, calling it the company's most powerful release yet for coding and agentic tasks, arriving just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash. The model targets engineers looking to speed up routine coding and reduce code-generation errors.

Gemini 3.7 Flash enters a crowded AI-coding segment alongside GitHub Copilot and Anthropic's Claude Code. The rapid 3.6-to-3.7 cadence underscores how compressed frontier release cycles have become.

Poland Becomes First EU Member State to Bring AI Act Implementing Law Into Force

Poland's AI Act implementing law entered into force on 11 August, making it the only member state to have done so. Two weeks after the European Commission gained the power to fine general-purpose AI providers, it has used none of it.

Poland is building a brand-new regulator, KRiBSI - the commission for the development and security of artificial intelligence - which will serve as the single market surveillance authority under the AI Act, drawing members from the competition, financial, broadcasting and telecoms regulators. The regulator does not yet exist, however, even as the law is live - a gap that highlights how uneven AI Act enforcement across the bloc remains.

Source: AI in Europe, 17 August 2026

California's AI Labeling Law Takes Effect Amid Missing Detection Tools

California's AI transparency and labeling law has now taken effect, requiring disclosure of AI-generated content. Compliance Week reports the rollout is being tested by the absence of reliable detection tools to verify labelling.

The law pushes platforms and creators to flag synthetic media, but enforcement hinges on technical capabilities - watermarking and provenance signals - that are not yet uniformly deployed. LMSYS Releases Miles v0.1, a Production-Grade RL Post-Training Stack

LMSYS Org unveiled Miles v0.1, a full-stack production-ready system for frontier post-training and the successor to its first Miles release. It optimises every stage of the RL training loop around verified, clean and customizable principles.

Miles ships maintained end-to-end recipes for agentic coding and terminal tasks, validated on real hardware. It supports two training backends behind one interface - NVIDIA Megatron-LM and PyTorch FSDP - selectable via a single launch flag, with Megatron-LM as the default offering tensor, pipeline, context and expert parallelism plus CPU and NVMe optimizer offload. The goal is to make frontier-scale reinforcement learning accessible to researchers without bespoke infrastructure.

ByteDance Seed and Tsinghua AIR Introduce CUDA Agent for Kernel Generation

ByteDance Seed and Tsinghua AIR introduced CUDA Agent, a large-scale agentic reinforcement-learning system for generating CUDA kernels. The system targets one of the hardest optimisation problems in GPU computing - writing efficient custom kernels.

Hand-writing high-performance CUDA kernels is a specialist skill in short supply. By framing kernel generation as an agentic RL problem, the system can explore, verify and iterate on kernel candidates at scale, potentially automating a bottleneck in AI infrastructure performance. The collaboration pairs ByteDance's production-scale compute with Tsinghua's academic research depth.

Amazon Bedrock Adds Auto-Generated Filters to Improve Contract Search Accuracy

AWS introduced auto-generated filters in Amazon Bedrock to improve retrieval accuracy in contract search workflows. The feature automatically derives metadata filters from user queries to narrow retrieval before generation.

The update targets a known RAG weak point: unstructured queries against large document corpora often return noisy results. By auto-generating structured filters - date ranges, parties, contract type - the system constrains the retrieval set, which a 2026 legal-RAG benchmark found matters more than the choice of LLM. One benchmark cited swapping embedding models lifted retrieval accuracy by 34 points while the LLM moved the needle far less.

Source: AWS Machine Learning Blog, August 2026

MiniMax Open-Sources H3, a Video-With-Sound Generation Model

MiniMax released the weights for H3, a multimodal generation model that produces video with native sound built in rather than added afterwards. The model landed on Hugging Face on 13 August and has been downloaded more than 2.3 million times.

H3 generates video with native 32 kHz stereo audio, 4 to 15 seconds long at 24 frames per second, with 2K resolution available through a separate regeneration step. The weights arrived roughly two weeks after the 31 July launch, when MiniMax committed to opening them subject to applicable laws. Native audio-video generation removes the sync and quality problems of stitching separate audio and video pipelines together.

Source: DEV Community, 17 August 2026
