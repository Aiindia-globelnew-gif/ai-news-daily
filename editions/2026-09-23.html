OpenAI launches GPT-6 Sol and Luna, cutting API prices in half

OpenAI expanded its GPT-6 family with Sol and Luna, two models trained with the same methods as the flagship GPT-6 Astra but priced 50% below the GPT-5.6 promotional rates — Sol at $2/$10 per million input/output tokens and Luna at $0.10/$0.50. The release landed just 90 minutes after Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5, underscoring how cost efficiency has become the new frontier battleground even as both labs' leaders call for slower development.

OpenAI says the new models make about half as many factual mistakes as their predecessors, with GPT-6 Sol reaching Astra-level reliability at much lower cost. On Zapier's AutomationBench, GPT-6 Sol scored 33.2% at its highest reasoning setting for $0.27 per task; on OSWorld 2.0 computer-use tasks it hit 60.5% at roughly 80% lower cost than Claude Opus 5 at medium effort. Improvements in caching and inference drive the price cuts, with discounts of up to 90% on cached input tokens. Both models are live in ChatGPT Work and Codex for paid tiers, with Luna also reaching free users via the desktop app; neither is in the main ChatGPT chat surface yet.

Source: OpenAI / TechCrunch, 22 September 2026

  • SenseTime's 8K image model - SenseTime released the production version of SenseNova U1 Pro, an image-creation model with up to 8K output, custom aspect ratios and an interleaved text-image chain of thought for structured, readable infographics. (TechNode, 22 September 2026)
