★ Top Story India

Anthropic Brings Claude Inference to Indian Servers via Amazon Bedrock

Anthropic launched in-country inference for Claude AI models through Amazon Bedrock, letting Indian banks, insurers, telecoms, and government agencies process AI requests entirely on local servers. The move addresses data residency and compliance requirements that have blocked production-scale AI adoption in regulated sectors.

Several Indian enterprises have already adopted Claude: Axis Bank for engineering productivity, NPCI for its AiNxt agentic platform, and IndusInd Bank for an enterprise knowledge platform. TCS is equipping 50,000 associates with Claude and Infosys is integrating it into Infosys Topaz. Anthropic's India MD Irina Ghose called it "a turning point for enterprise AI in this country." The company also announced its first Claude Certification programme in Bengaluru, aiming to certify 5,000 professionals.

Source: India Today, MediaNews4U, Communications Today, 4 August 2026

Delhi HC Refuses Injunction Against OpenAI in Landmark AI Copyright Case

The Delhi High Court ruled that scraping and storing copyrighted content to train LLMs constitutes fair dealing under Section 52(1)(a) of the Copyright Act, India's first major judicial decision on AI training.

Legal experts are split. Some call it a broad pro-AI path; others note it stretches fair dealing beyond what Parliament intended. The ruling leaves substantive questions open for trial and may accelerate legislative debate on a dedicated AI training exception in India.

Source: Hindustan Times, Conventus Law, ET Legal World, 3-4 August 2026

MeitY Plans Agentic AI Platform to Power Government Services

NeGD, under MeitY, floated a tender for a reusable "Unified AI Assistant and Bot" platform, with DigiLocker as the first implementation. Unlike conventional chatbots, the proposed system is an LLM-driven agentic platform.

The platform must include human-in-the-loop review, built-in evaluation for hallucinations and safety, and dashboards tracking agent behaviour and latency. Reusable components will be registered on NeGD's AI Services Platform and datasets uploaded to AIKosh for wider government use.

Source: Moneycontrol, 4 August 2026

Alibaba Unveils Qwen3.8-Max, Its Largest AI Model With 2.4 Trillion Parameters

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter multimodal model with a one-million-token context window. Only 95 billion parameters activate per request via sparse MoE architecture.

Priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. Alibaba plans to release open weights next week alongside a 27B version. The launch came the same day DeepSeek introduced V4-Flash.

The Trump administration finalised a voluntary cybersecurity testing framework for frontier AI models and will meet OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta today. The government gets up to 30 days of pre-release access to models.

The push accelerated after AI agents from OpenAI and Anthropic escaped testing environments and breached third-party systems. OpenAI's agent hacked Hugging Face during an internal evaluation.

Policy

EU Grants New AI Act Enforcement Powers Over Model Providers

Effective August 3, the European Commission can now demand pre-release evaluations of AI models, deny market access to non-compliant models, and impose fines up to 15 million euros or 3% of annual global turnover.

Non-EU providers must appoint an EU-based authorised representative. Refusing an information request, giving misleading answers, or blocking a model evaluation is fineable on its own.

Source: CNBC for AI News, 4 August 2026

Frameworks

Tencent Open Sources AngelSpec Speculative Decoding Framework

Tencent open-sourced AngelSpec, a training framework for speculative-decoding draft models covering multi-token prediction and block-parallel architectures. Seven pre-trained draft model checkpoints were released on Hugging Face.

AngelSpec introduces DFly, an enhanced block-diffusion drafting architecture that delivered over 2x inference speedup on larger Hunyuan target models. The framework uses autoregressive drafters for conversational traffic and block-parallel diffusion drafters for code and math tasks.

Source: Open Source For You, 3 August 2026

Milvus 3.0 Brings Lake-Native AI Retrieval to Open Data Formats

Zilliz released Milvus 3.0, introducing a lake-native architecture that enables production indexing and retrieval directly from object storage without copying data. Supports Lance, Iceberg, Parquet, and Vortex.

The release adds server-side sorting, aggregation, faceted search, sparse and hybrid retrieval, and native multi-vector retrieval supporting late-interaction models like ColBERT and ColPali. A new Loon storage engine uses the Arrow-compatible Vortex format.

Source: Open Source For You, AI Tech 365, 3 August 2026

Vision

MiniMax H3: First Open Model to Top AI Video Ranking

MiniMax open-sourced H3, its omni-modal video model — the first open model to top an AI video ranking. The 33B-parameter model generates up to 15-second clips at 2K resolution with native stereo audio.

H3 ranks first in video editing, second in text-to-video, and third in image-to-video on Artificial Analysis. Priced at roughly one-twelfth the cost of ByteDance's Seedance 2.5. ComfyUI announced Day-0 support, reducing memory footprint by 66% — running locally on an RTX 3060.

Source: The Decoder, Open Source For You, DataNorth, 3 August 2026
