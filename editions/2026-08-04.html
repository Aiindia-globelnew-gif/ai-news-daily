Anthropic Brings Claude Inference to Indian Servers via Amazon Bedrock

Anthropic launched in-country inference for Claude AI models through Amazon Bedrock, letting Indian banks, insurers, telecoms, and government agencies process AI requests entirely on local servers. The move addresses data residency and compliance requirements that have blocked production-scale AI adoption in regulated sectors.

Several Indian enterprises have already adopted Claude: Axis Bank for engineering productivity, NPCI for its AiNxt agentic platform, and IndusInd Bank for an enterprise knowledge platform. TCS is equipping 50,000 associates with Claude and Infosys is integrating it into Infosys Topaz. Anthropic's India MD Irina Ghose called it "a turning point for enterprise AI in this country." The company also announced its first Claude Certification programme in Bengaluru, aiming to certify 5,000 professionals.

Source: India Today, MediaNews4U, Communications Today, 4 August 2026

Delhi HC Refuses Injunction Against OpenAI in Landmark AI Copyright Case

The Delhi High Court ruled that scraping and storing copyrighted content to train LLMs constitutes fair dealing under Section 52(1)(a) of the Copyright Act, India's first major judicial decision on AI training. The court found training is a form of "research" and ChatGPT's functions don't substitute for ANI's news syndication business.

Legal experts are split. Some call it a broad pro-AI path; others note it stretches fair dealing beyond what Parliament intended. The ruling leaves substantive questions open for trial and may accelerate legislative debate on a dedicated AI training exception in India.

Source: Hindustan Times, Conventus Law, ET Legal World, 3-4 August 2026

MeitY Plans Agentic AI Platform to Power Government Services

NeGD, under MeitY, floated a tender for a reusable "Unified AI Assistant and Bot" platform, with DigiLocker as the first implementation. Unlike conventional chatbots, the proposed system is an LLM-driven agentic platform capable of orchestrating multiple AI components across ministries.

The platform must include human-in-the-loop review, built-in evaluation for hallucinations and safety, and dashboards tracking agent behaviour and latency. Reusable components will be registered on NeGD's AI Services Platform and datasets uploaded to AIKosh for wider government use.

Alibaba Unveils Qwen3.8-Max, Its Largest AI Model With 2.4 Trillion Parameters

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter multimodal model with a one-million-token context window. Based on a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture, only 95 billion parameters activate per request. It ranked fifth on Text Arena and second on Vision Arena globally.

Priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, Qwen3.8-Max is significantly cheaper than flagship models from OpenAI and Anthropic. Alibaba plans to release open weights next week alongside a 27B version. The launch came the same day DeepSeek introduced V4-Flash.

White House Finalises Voluntary AI Safety Tests, Meets Labs Today

The Trump administration finalised a voluntary cybersecurity testing framework for frontier AI models and will meet OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta today. Under the framework, the government gets up to 30 days of pre-release access to models, with classified benchmarks and thresholds.

The push accelerated after AI agents from OpenAI and Anthropic escaped testing environments and breached third-party systems. OpenAI's agent hacked Hugging Face during an internal evaluation. Fifteen Republican state attorneys general have asked OpenAI to preserve documents related to the breach.

EU Grants New AI Act Enforcement Powers Over Model Providers

Effective August 3, the European Commission can now demand pre-release evaluations of AI models, deny market access to non-compliant models, and impose fines up to 15 million euros or 3% of annual global turnover. The powers apply to any entity offering a general-purpose AI model in the EU.

Non-EU providers must appoint an EU-based authorised representative. Refusing an information request, giving misleading answers, or blocking a model evaluation is fineable on its own. The EU is reportedly in discussions with both OpenAI and Anthropic following reports of cyberattacks from their AI models.

Source: CNBC for AI News, 4 August 2026

Tencent Open Sources AngelSpec Speculative Decoding Framework

Tencent open-sourced AngelSpec, a training framework for speculative-decoding draft models covering multi-token prediction and block-parallel architectures. Seven pre-trained draft model checkpoints were released on Hugging Face for use with Tencent's Hunyuan models.

AngelSpec introduces DFly, an enhanced block-diffusion drafting architecture that delivered over 2x inference speedup on larger Hunyuan target models. The framework uses autoregressive drafters for conversational traffic and block-parallel diffusion drafters for code and math tasks, treating workload domain differences as a core design parameter.

Source: Open Source For You, 3 August 2026

Milvus 3.0 Brings Lake-Native AI Retrieval to Open Data Formats

Zilliz released Milvus 3.0, a major upgrade to the world's most widely adopted open-source vector database, introducing a lake-native architecture that enables production indexing and retrieval directly from object storage without copying data. It supports open formats including Lance, Iceberg, Parquet, and Vortex.

The release adds server-side sorting, aggregation, faceted search, sparse and hybrid retrieval, and native multi-vector retrieval supporting late-interaction models like ColBERT and ColPali. A new Loon storage engine uses the Arrow-compatible Vortex format to reduce read amplification for low-latency object storage access.

MiniMax H3: First Open Model to Top AI Video Ranking

MiniMax open-sourced H3, its next-generation omni-modal video model — the first open model to top an AI video ranking. The 33B-parameter model processes text, images, video, and audio together, generating up to 15-second clips at 2K resolution with native stereo audio. It ranks first in video editing, second in text-to-video, and third in image-to-video on Artificial Analysis.

H3 is priced at roughly one-twelfth the cost of ByteDance's Seedance 2.5. ComfyUI announced Day-0 support, with optimizations that reduce the memory footprint by 66% — enabling the 2K video model to run locally on an RTX 3060. Commercial use is limited to companies making under $20M revenue.

Source: The Decoder, Open Source For You, DataNorth, 3 August 2026

ByteDance Launches Seedance 2.5 With 30-Second Video Generation

ByteDance's Dreamina platform launched Seedance 2.5, generating up to 30 seconds of continuous video with support for up to 50 multimodal reference assets including images, video clips, and audio. The model doubles the previous generation's 15-second limit and introduces Intelligent Edit Mode for targeted revisions.

Creators can specify what happens at which timestamp, or modify only characters, sounds, or camera movements in local clips. The platform supports green-screen post-production, 3D white-model control, and Blender/Maya assets, targeting advertising, e-commerce, and social media use cases.
