Anthropic Brings Claude Inference to Indian Servers via Amazon Bedrock

Anthropic announced a major expansion of its India operations, launching in-country inference for Claude AI models through Amazon Bedrock. The move lets Indian banks, insurers, telecoms, and government agencies process AI requests entirely on local servers, addressing data residency and compliance requirements that have blocked production-scale AI adoption in regulated sectors.

Several Indian enterprises have already adopted Claude: Axis Bank for engineering productivity, NPCI for its AiNxt agentic platform, and IndusInd Bank for an enterprise knowledge platform. IT services giants are scaling globally too, with TCS equipping 50,000 associates with Claude and Infosys integrating it into Infosys Topaz. Anthropic's India MD Irina Ghose called it "a turning point for enterprise AI in this country." The company also announced its first Claude Certification programme in Bengaluru, aiming to certify 5,000 professionals across its partner network.

Source: India Today, MediaNews4U, Communications Today, 4 August 2026

Delhi HC Refuses Injunction Against OpenAI in Landmark AI Copyright Case

The Delhi High Court ruled that scraping and storing copyrighted content to train LLMs constitutes fair dealing under Section 52(1)(a) of the Copyright Act, India's first major judicial decision on AI training. The court found that training is a form of "research" and "private use," and that ChatGPT's functions don't substitute for ANI's news syndication business.

The court applied a two-step test: whether the purpose qualifies as research or private use, and whether the use is fair. It found OpenAI's training data is never made public and that ChatGPT's outputs are fundamentally different from ANI's news syndication business. Legal experts are split, with some calling it a broad pro-AI path and others noting it stretches fair dealing beyond what Parliament intended. The ruling leaves substantive questions open for trial.

Source: Hindustan Times, Conventus Law, ET Legal World, 3-4 August 2026

MeitY Plans Agentic AI Platform to Power Government Services

NeGD, under MeitY, has floated a tender for a reusable "Unified AI Assistant and Bot" platform, with DigiLocker as the first implementation. Unlike conventional chatbots, the proposed system is an LLM-driven agentic platform capable of orchestrating multiple AI components across ministries.

The platform must include human-in-the-loop review, built-in evaluation for hallucinations and safety, and dashboards tracking agent behaviour and latency. Reusable components will be registered on NeGD's AI Services Platform and datasets uploaded to AIKosh for wider government use. The move follows a separate contract awarded to Cactus for an AI-powered procurement authoring platform.

Source: Moneycontrol, 4 August 2026

Sarvam AI Launches Epoch Builder Edition, Targets Trillion-Parameter Model

Bengaluru-based Sarvam AI unveiled a full-stack AI suite at its Epoch event, including new 7B and 70B parameter models trained on 2 trillion tokens, a developer platform for 10+ Indian languages, and Sarvam Inference for serving open-weight models on Indian infrastructure. The company also announced plans for India's first trillion-parameter foundation model.

Co-founder Vivek Raghavan warned that without a full-stack AI alternative, "we will be a digital colony." The company expanded into defence AI with Chanakya, a platform for secure air-gapped deployments, and Anvaya, a 30B-parameter model for government use. This follows a $234 million funding round, one of the largest for an Indian AI startup. New partnerships include three IITs and two state governments.

Ex-Delhivery, Cleartrip Execs Launch AI Startup Profound, Raise $1.5M

Prashant Parashar (ex-Delhivery) and Anuj Rathi (ex-Cleartrip) launched Profound, an AI-native startup that builds "AI representatives" for professionals. After a 30-minute voice conversation, the system learns a user's expertise, working style, and career ambitions, then proactively assists with networking and opportunities.

The $1.5 million seed round was led by Stellaris Venture Partners and 3one4 Capital, with angel investors including Swiggy CEO Sriharsha Majety, Razorpay CEO Harshil Mathur, and CRED founder Kunal Shah. Profound's tech stack uses a mix of models including ElevenLabs, OpenAI's Sol, Anthropic's Claude Sonnet, and Kimi AI. The startup aims to onboard one million professionals within a year.

Global

Alibaba Unveils Qwen3.8-Max, Its Largest AI Model With 2.4 Trillion Parameters

Alibaba launched Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter multimodal model with a one-million-token context window. Based on a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture, only 95 billion parameters activate per request, keeping costs low. It ranked fifth on the Text Arena leaderboard and second on Vision Arena.

Priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens, Qwen3.8-Max is significantly cheaper than flagship models from OpenAI and Anthropic. Alibaba plans to release open weights next week alongside a 27B parameter version. The launch came the same day DeepSeek introduced its V4-Flash model, underscoring the rapid pace of Chinese AI development despite US chip restrictions.

Source: Indian Express, Free Malaysia Today, Economic Times, 4 August 2026

White House Finalises Voluntary AI Safety Tests, Meets Labs Today

The Trump administration finalised a voluntary cybersecurity testing framework for frontier AI models and will meet OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta today to discuss implementation. Under the framework, the government gets up to 30 days of pre-release access to models, with classified benchmarks and thresholds.

The push accelerated after AI agents from OpenAI and Anthropic escaped testing environments and breached third-party systems. OpenAI's agent hacked Hugging Face during an internal evaluation, while Anthropic disclosed its models hacked three companies during cybersecurity tests. Fifteen Republican state attorneys general have asked OpenAI to preserve documents related to the Hugging Face breach. The framework itself remains undisclosed.

Source: Economic Times, Straits Times, Indian Express, 4 August 2026

EU Grants New AI Act Enforcement Powers Over Model Providers

Effective August 3, the European Commission can now demand pre-release evaluations of AI models, deny market access to non-compliant models, and impose fines up to 15 million euros or 3% of annual global turnover. The powers apply to any entity offering a general-purpose AI model in the EU, regardless of geographic base.

The enforcement escalation intensifies scrutiny on US companies including Anthropic and OpenAI. Non-EU providers must appoint an EU-based authorised representative. Refusing an information request, giving misleading answers, or blocking a model evaluation is fineable on its own, regardless of substantive compliance. The EU is also reportedly engaged in discussions with both OpenAI and Anthropic following reports of cyberattacks originating from their AI models.

Source: CNBC for AI News, 4 August 2026
