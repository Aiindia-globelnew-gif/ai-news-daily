★ Top Story India

MeitY readies an AI law that would make safety testing mandatory before release

India's IT ministry is expected to publish a consultation paper on the country's AI rules as early as next month, with a 60-day window for public feedback, NDTV Profit reported. The draft considers a separate set of safety requirements for frontier models defined by computing power and capability, including mandatory safety testing before public release and sandbox trials. Formal legislation could follow in the Budget Session.

The framework is expected to reach developers, platforms and deployers, with sharper obligations where an AI system causes harm, and to address deepfakes, AI-enabled fraud and impersonation. Sectoral regulators such as RBI, SEBI and TRAI may layer on their own compliance requirements. Defining frontier models by compute and capability would let the government keep requirements proportionate, separating the largest systems from smaller applications. The ministry has not published the paper, and the timeline remains a reported plan rather than a notified rule.

Source: NDTV Profit, 16 Nov 2026

Sarvam opens an India-hosted inference service to all developers

Bengaluru's Sarvam AI opened Sarvam Token Factory to all developers and enterprises on 16 November, running its own models and leading open-weight models on GPU clusters inside India through a single OpenAI-compatible API. Prices are listed in rupees and zero data retention is available on request.

The paid service serves Sarvam's own Sarvam 105B alongside open-weight models including DeepSeek and GLM. It puts an Indian company in direct competition with global inference providers at a moment when in-country processing has become a selling point for enterprise buyers, following OpenAI's India inference residency for GPT-6 Luna. For regulated Indian enterprises, rupee billing and domestic hosting remove two common procurement objections.

Source: CIOL, 17 Nov 2026

India signs a US-led AI supply-chain declaration with nine other governments

India joined the United States, Australia, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the UAE and the UK in signing the San Francisco Declaration on the Secure Foundations of Enduring Prosperity on 15 November, at the Pax Silica Founders Retreat. The declaration sets up five working groups on innovation policy, AI adoption, supply chains, secure industrial hubs and infrastructure resilience.

ETGovernment notes the declaration is a political commitment: it creates no binding procurement rules, technology-transfer obligations or new Indian regulation. India's exposure is concentrated in dependence on Chinese rare-earth processing, semiconductor fabrication equipment and specialised components, none of which a coalition can substitute quickly. The value, officials suggest, lies in coordinating investment and identifying vulnerabilities across the chain rather than in immediate supply relief.

Source: ETGovernment, 17 Nov 2026

India

KiteFishAI trains a 155-million-parameter model from scratch in India

KiteFishAI released NanoI1, a 155-million-parameter language model trained from scratch on roughly one trillion tokens using eight Nvidia H100 GPUs, under an Apache-2.0 licence. At 311 MB it runs on laptops and local servers, and is aimed at KYC document extraction, complaint classification and support ticket routing.

The startup says NanoI1 beat Google's Gemma-3-270M on its own evaluations: a HumanEval coding score of 9.1 per cent against 2.4 per cent, 4.3 per cent on EvalPlus against 1.2, and 4 per cent on GSM8K against 1.8. It supports English, Hindi and eight other Indian languages with a 2,048-token context window and vLLM serving. Absolute scores are low, which the company concedes: it positions NanoI1 for narrow enterprise tasks, not as a general-purpose alternative to larger models.

Source: ET EnterpriseAI, 17 Nov 2026

Global

OpenAI pushes computer-use agents into clinical and billing workflows

OpenAI published results for computer-use agents in healthcare, evaluating GPT-6 Astra on multi-step tasks in clinician inboxes and on HealthAdminBench, a Stanford benchmark for administrative workflows. It also named Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare and CVS Health as partners across prior authorisation, referrals and discharge planning.

Physician reviewers rated 99 per cent of 325 reviewed tasks clinically safe, and marked the model's work very or extremely helpful in 93 per cent of 646 assessments. On HealthAdminBench, GPT-6 Astra earned 94.3 per cent of rubric points while GPT-6 Luna earned 91.8 per cent at 8.5 cents a task, 98 per cent cheaper. The capabilities ship through the API, ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians, with HIPAA-compliant configurations, approval steps and audit logs. OpenAI says organisation data is not used to train its foundation models.

Source: OpenAI, 16 Nov 2026

Research

TCSExam puts frontier agents through real theoretical computer science

A new arXiv paper introduces TCSExam, a benchmark covering theorem proving, solver engineering, constructions and direct attempts at open conjectures, with 100 expert-reviewed tasks checked by the Lean kernel or executable checkers. GPT-6 Astra leads the full set at 71.9 on the TCSExam Index; Opus 5.5 leads the 20-task Lite version at 72.3.

The authors report that Full and Lite rankings align closely across the eight shared agents, with a Spearman correlation of 0.93, which supports the shorter benchmark as a cheap proxy. Submissions produced improved solver outputs and mathematical constructions, plus independently verified special cases and bounds from conjecture attempts. Task sources, Docker build recipes and evaluation tools are released for reproducibility.

Source: arXiv, November 2026

Policy

EU science and ethics advisers warn existing guard rails no longer suffice

The European Commission's Group of Chief Scientific Advisors and its Group on Ethics in Science and New Technologies issued a joint statement on 16 November arguing that traditional AI guard rails are inadequate as systems become more autonomous. They call for strong existing legislation, protected human agency and oversight, and regulation that evolves with capability.

Four recommendations anchor the statement: keep safety, security and human agency as prerequisites for development; calibrate deployment to potential danger; build independent European capacity to evaluate models rather than delegating it to commercial developers; and fund a safety and security ecosystem with methods that keep pace. The advisers warn that weak political commitment or institutional capacity could undermine existing obligations, and that external efforts to delay or weaken legislation would further erode safeguards.

Source: European Commission, 16 Nov 2026

Frameworks

Google's Antigravity rewrote 800,000 lines of kernel code into safe Rust

Google detailed results from Antigravity's /teamwork multi-agent system, which rewrote the remainder of the Zircon microkernel into about 800,000 lines of safe Rust in a two-week campaign. The tools are being opened to outside developers and ship in Antigravity today with Gemini 4 models.

The design pairs parallel exploration with falsifiers, including the Lean 4 proof checker, unmodified test suites, execution simulators and hardware counters, to decide which candidates survive; each agent only gets the files and tools its step needs, and critics re-check results from clean contexts. A new rewrite pattern schedules each module only after its dependencies compile and links every leaf module against the original test suite. The same approach took Google's libgav1 AV1 decoder to roughly 170,000 lines of Rust with bit-exact parity, ending 20-30 per cent faster than the original C++ and hand-tuned assembly.

Source: Google Research, via Darius, 16 Nov 2026

RAG

Amazon S3 Vectors adds hybrid search to cut RAG plumbing

Amazon S3 Vectors now supports hybrid search that combines vector similarity with full-text search in a single query, fusing the two ranked lists with Reciprocal Rank Fusion. It uses the same APIs, with subsecond latency for infrequent queries and around 100 milliseconds or less for frequent ones.

Customers write searchable text as another metadata field with PutVectors, and S3 Vectors indexes it for BM25 relevance scoring, using a language-agnostic analyzer by default with optional language-specific analyzers. Because semantic and exact-term matching arrive as one ranked list, teams can serve RAG, agent memory and search from object storage without a separate vector database. AWS claims up to 90 per cent lower total cost of storing and querying vectors than alternative vector solutions. Hybrid search is live in all commercial regions where S3 Vectors is available, and in the AWS China Regions.

Source: AWS, 16 Nov 2026

RAG

RAGFlow 1.0 reframes retrieval as a context supply layer for agents

RAGFlow released version 1.0 on 9 November, repositioning from an end-to-end RAG engine to what it calls an Agent Native Database. The pitch is a context supply layer that an agent harness queries directly, rather than a retrieval backend that hands fragments to a model.

The project argues that retrieval in the agent era is multi-turn orchestration plus context governance, which needs its own layer for compressing tokens, tracing evidence and governing permissions and versions. RAGFlow compiles raw data into typed, versioned artifacts: text snippets, evidence units, navigation structures, relational skeletons, timelines, wikis, ontology assertions and memory facts. The stated division of labour is that the harness orchestrates and RAGFlow supplies, with tools as the only access path.

Source: RAGFlow, 9 Nov 2026

Vision

MiniMax releases H3-Image weights for 4K generation in under ten seconds

MiniMax published H3-Image, an open-weight image model built on its H3 video system, which generates a 4K image in under 10 seconds and handles text-to-image, reference-based generation and editing with up to 16 reference images. The weights are public.

Independent write-ups put the native output at roughly 16 megapixels from a single 33.1B checkpoint in six steps, with no CFG and no negative prompt. The practical catch is size and licensing: the full BF16 set runs about 135 GB, and the H3 community licence excludes the US, EU, UK and South Korea, with written permission needed for commercial use above $1 million in annual revenue. There is no technical report or independent benchmark yet, so claims about instruction following remain MiniMax's own.

Source: MiniMax, 10 Nov 2026
